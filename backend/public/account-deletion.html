Delete your Studio Agents account

You can permanently delete your Studio Agents account and associated creative data from the signed-in app:

  1. Sign in to Studio Agents.
  2. Open Account or Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account, review the confirmation, and submit.

The authenticated deletion workflow removes the Firebase account, profile, generations, projects, uploaded assets, and stored voice references where available. Some records may be retained where required by law or for security and fraud prevention.

If you cannot sign in, email privacy@studioagentsai.com from the account email and include “Account deletion request”. We will verify ownership before processing the request.
