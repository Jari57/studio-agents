Delete your account

Studio Agents account holders can permanently delete their profile, projects, uploaded assets, generations, and Firebase authentication account from the signed-in Settings area.

How to delete

Open Studio Agents, sign in, open Settings, choose the Account or Danger Zone section, and select Delete Account. Confirm the warning to finish deletion. This action cannot be undone.

Can’t sign in?
